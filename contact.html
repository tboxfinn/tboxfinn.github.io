03 — FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What types of projects do you work on?

I work on a variety of game projects including mobile games, PC/console titles, VR experiences, and educational games. I'm particularly interested in projects that push creative boundaries or solve interesting design challenges.

Do you work with indie teams and studios?

Absolutely! I love working with indie teams and have experience scaling my approach for teams of all sizes. Whether you're a solo developer or a mid-sized studio, I can adapt my workflow to fit your needs and budget.

What's your typical project timeline?

Project timelines vary greatly depending on scope and complexity. Design consultations might take a few weeks, while full game development can range from a few months to over a year. I always provide detailed timeline estimates during initial discussions.

Do you offer mentoring or educational services?

Yes! I occasionally take on mentoring students and junior developers, and I'm available for workshops, talks, and educational consultations. If you're interested in learning game design, feel free to reach out.
